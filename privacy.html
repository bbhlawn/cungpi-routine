Privacy Policy

Cungpi's Routine · Last updated September 4, 2026

Cungpi's Routine does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app is designed for a child to use offline, with no accounts and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. The app has no login, no sign-up, and does not gather analytics or usage data. We do not use advertising or any third-party data-collection services.

Camera & photos

The optional "Wink Wonk" activity lets the child take a fun selfie. Any photo taken is saved only on the device, inside the app. Photos are never uploaded to us or any server. A parent may choose to share a photo using the phone's standard share sheet (for example, to Messages); that action is entirely under the family's control.

Data storage

Routine progress, calendar events, settings, and photos are stored locally on the device. Deleting the app removes this data.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for use by children with a parent or guardian. Because the app collects no data, there is nothing transmitted or sold. It contains no ads and no external links or purchases.
