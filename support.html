Cungpi's Routine — Support

A friendly morning & night routine helper for kids.

How it works

  • Pick Morning or Night at the top.
  • Tap Start my day (or Let's wind down) to go through each task one at a time.
  • Tap I did it! when a task is finished — you'll get a cheer and the next task appears.
  • During Wink Wonk, tap the camera to snap a silly selfie (saved on the device only).
  • The calendar (top-right) shows events and the photos taken each day.

Privacy

The app collects no data. Everything stays on the device. See our Privacy Policy.
